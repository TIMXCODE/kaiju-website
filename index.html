Shortcuts

Save clips without leaving the game.

Global hotkeys work while a game is fullscreen. Every action has two shortcuts out of the box — a ⌘⌥ combination and a Shift + function key (⇧F1–⇧F6). All of them are remappable.

⌘⌥CSave a clip from the buffer
⌘⌥ISave an instant replay — the shorter length
⌘⌥RStart or stop the replay buffer
⌘⌥HShow or hide Kaiju
⌘⌥MMute or unmute the microphone
⇧F6Mark a moment to jump to when you save

What is Kaiju? +

A native macOS app for instant-replay clip capture. It records your game continuously, saves up to the last thirty minutes on a hotkey, and gives you a library, a simple editor and size-aware export in the same window.

Which Macs are supported? +

Apple Silicon Macs on macOS 14 or later. Capture and export both run on the hardware media engine, so the replay buffer costs very little while you play.

Will Kaiju stay free? +

It is free during early access. After that there will be a paid version, and the intention is to keep it under $5 as a one-time purchase. Nothing is locked behind a subscription.

Can it compress a clip to a specific size? +

Yes. Choose a destination profile or type a target, and Kaiju exports a file that lands just under it at the best quality that fits.

Can I record my microphone? +

Game audio and microphone are captured with independent levels, so you can set the balance once and every clip records that way.

macOS says it cannot verify the app. Now what? +

Right-click Kaiju in Applications and choose Open the first time, or allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security. Notarisation is in progress for a later early-access build.
