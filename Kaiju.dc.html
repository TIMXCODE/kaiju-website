Questions

What is Kaiju? +

A Mac app for capturing, trimming and sharing gameplay clips. Record a session or let Instant Replay hold the last thirty seconds, then trim and export without opening anything else.

What Macs does Kaiju support? +

Apple silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later. Capture and export both use the hardware media engine, so recording costs very little while you play.

Does Kaiju have Instant Replay? +

Yes. Kaiju keeps a rolling buffer while you play; one shortcut writes it to your library as a finished clip.

Can Kaiju compress clips to a specific size? +

Pick a target — 10, 25, 50, 100, 250 MB or your own number — and Kaiju exports a file that lands just under it, at the best quality that fits.

Can I record my microphone? +

Game audio and microphone are recorded with independent levels, so you can set the balance once and forget about it.
