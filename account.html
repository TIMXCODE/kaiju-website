Help

I lost my license key

Your key was shown right after checkout and is tied to the email you paid with. Find your Stripe receipt (search your inbox for "Kaiju" or "Stripe") and ask on Discord or via GitHub — quote the receipt, never the card number.

"This license is already active on 3 Macs"

Look the key up above and deactivate a Mac you no longer use. The slot frees up immediately.

Does Kaiju work offline?

Yes. Once a Mac has verified its license, it keeps working for 7 days with no connection at all, and re-checks quietly when you're back online.

Do I need an account?

No. The license key is the whole system — there's no account, no password, and nothing tracked.

Refunds

14 days, no questions. Ask through Discord or GitHub with your receipt. A refund revokes the license.

Is Kaiju free right now?

Yes — during early access everything is unlocked and no key is needed. Keys bought today activate automatically if licensing ever switches on.
